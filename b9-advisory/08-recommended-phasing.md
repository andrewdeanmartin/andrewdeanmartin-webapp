## Stop / continue / expand gates

At month 6 of Phase 1+2:

### Stop signals
- Phase 1 use cases not adopted by team (signals change-management gap, not AI problem)
- Cost > $1,000/month with no measurable Phase 1 ROI
- Compliance incident or legal challenge on any use case (immediate halt + counsel)
- Brenda's strategic direction shifts (e.g., decides to sell B9 — reframe entire portfolio)
- Talent supply degradation traced to any AI use case (rare but possible — kill that use case immediately)

### Continue signals
- Phase 1 hitting baseline KPI targets
- No compliance issues
- Team adoption ≥70% on deployed use cases
- Brenda continues to invest 1-2 hours/week reviewing

### Expand signals
- Phase 1 exceeding KPI targets (move Phase 2 timing forward)
- Clear customer / talent demand for specific Phase 3 watchlist items
- Operating capacity freed up (the >>2x throughput pattern from peer benchmarks)
- New revenue tied to AI use cases (RFP wins, expansion captures, renewal saves)

## What this phasing protects against

| Risk | Mitigation in phasing |
|---|---|
| "Expand everything" trap | Branch-gated Phase 2 limits to 4-6 use cases, not 8 |
| Wrong-priority Phase 2 | Brenda's answers gate the Branch; no random sequencing |
| Compliance blowback | Phase 1 includes compliance redesigns + counsel review |
| Vendor commitment too early | L0-L1 patterns dominate; L3 commitments deferred to Phase 3 |
| Adoption failure | Phase 2 gates at month 6 force evaluation before more investment |
| Brenda burnout | Phase 1 follows existing 90-day plan she's already committed to; Phase 2 is opt-in |
| Talent supply collapse from AI overreach | Branch D specifically requires Brenda's affirmative consent for talent-side AI |
| Misaligned with B9 strategy | Q2 (Ambition) shapes Branch choice |

---

## What this phasing does NOT do

- **Doesn't fix change management.** Adoption depends on Brenda's team buying in. The portfolio enables AI; people implement it.
- **Doesn't replace legal review.** The 4 compliance-affected use cases need counsel sign-off.
- **Doesn't auto-prioritize watchlist.** Month 6 review is a human decision, not algorithmic.
- **Doesn't address strategic questions.** If Brenda's real question is "should I sell B9?" or "should I add a new service line?" — that's a different conversation outside this scope.
